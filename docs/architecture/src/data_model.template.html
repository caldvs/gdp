Transactions

  • New bill version: put V#nnn and LATEST, and the IMGHASH# row on first parse.
  • Create session: META + every PART# row, plus JCODE# and BILLLOCK#, both create-if-absent.
  • Claim: the diner's PART# row, conditional on CLAIMS' version; on a clash the API re-reads and re-checks.
  • Amend mid-session: new bill version + LATEST + reconciled META and participant rows, removed lines' SHARE# rows deleted, CLAIMS bumped.
  • Lock claims: META to claiming and the creator's PART# row together.
  • Split change: SHARE# row (guarded by version), the diner's PART# row, and CLAIMS (guarded when joining or leaving).
  • Payment update: PAY# row and the payer's PART# row; mark-paid is conditional on pending.
  • Close and abandon: delete BILLLOCK#. Guests keep reading a closed bill through their GSI1 sessions.

Lifetimes

  • Kept: users, auth identities, bills, image hashes, payments, table fees, venue lookups.
  • 30 days: sessions (META, PART#, SHARE#, CLAIMS), join codes, bill locks.
  • Short-lived: sign-in codes 10 min, idempotency 24 h, bill reads 1 day.
