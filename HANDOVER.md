## State of main

- **The app** is the Option D web UI. The whole flow is built: scan → check the bill → bank details → platform fee (when Stripe is configured) → QR → guests claim and split → pay the lead → lead closes → everyone's emailed a report. See the [README](README.md) and [decisions](docs/decisions.md).
- **Bill reading** is Claude Sonnet 4.6 on Bedrock (both calls: finding the receipt and reading it) and nothing else: no fallback reader, no Google services. Locally it uses your `AWS_PROFILE`. The app uploads photos up to 3200px; the API asks the model where the receipt is, crops to it, and sends a long one as up to 4 overlapping parts in one call (`receiptImages.ts`, pure-JS `jpeg-js`). The model copies each line's printed total and quantity; the API works out unit prices (`splitTotal`, two lines when it doesn't divide evenly). Minus lines (deals, comps) are folded into the item they came from, or shared by price when the reader can't tell which (`discounts.ts`); deposits are left out and the total is the pre-deposit one. Repeated lines (four "Zinfandel BTL £21.50" rows) become one line with that quantity (`mergeRepeatedLines` in `parseService.ts`: same name ignoring case and spacing, price each, category and course; `unsure` lines stay apart). It runs in the background: the app uploads, calls `POST /v1/bills/reads`, and the Lambda invokes itself to read (45s budget). `/reading/[id]` polls it and opens Review bill when done; the lead can go back to Bills, which shows the read as a row (kept in this browser's storage). A failed read keeps the photo with *Try again* (same upload, no re-upload) and *New photo*. The synchronous `POST /v1/bills/parse` still works, with 22s to fit API Gateway's 30s (504 `PARSE_TIMEOUT` when it runs out).
- **Venue:** Bedrock reads the name and printed address off the bill; Amazon Location Service then fills in the full address and coordinates (`VENUE_LOOKUP=amazon-location` when deployed and locally via `.env.example`, using your `AWS_PROFILE`; `none` in tests and CI). Lookups are cached for good in `VENUEQ#` rows. The lookup only counts when it finds a point of interest with exactly the printed name (`sameVenueName`); otherwise the bill keeps what's printed, and a bill with no name gets nothing from it. Rows from before carry no `exact` flag and are looked up again. Review bill shows the venue as one row (name only; the looked-up address isn't shown, as it starts with the name). **Tax:** UK prices include VAT; when items + service already make the printed total, the bill's tax is 0 whatever the reader said (`addedTaxPence`, #176). **Prompt v9:** a dish priced on the line under a 0.00 line is one item; admin fees and surcharges are service; lines it can't read clearly get an `unsure` note (on `BillItem`, cleared by any amend); JSON is found even with words around it. **v10** (7 Nov): a charity line ("#CookForUkraine 1.00") is an item, and a "discount" that only makes the printed total when added on is turned back into a charge (`asCharges` in `billPrompt.ts`); the venue's name is never expanded from initials ("PKB").
- **Check these (#107):** before Review bill, the lead confirms each `unsure` line one at a time (`Check this`, with the photo), then *Where was this?* when the receipt names no place (Skip is remembered per tab in sessionStorage; Review bill has *add place*). One amend at the end. Not shown mid-session.

- **Splits on longer lines:** some of a line with more than one of something can be split (2 of 4 bottles three ways). The split's `units` live on its `SHARE#` row (absent = 1 on older splits) and come back on `shares[].units`; `POST /shares` takes `units` when starting (default: every unit nobody has claimed). The rest of the line is still claimed one at a time, and claims, amends and completeness count the split's units. On the claim list the split is its own row (`<itemId>:split`) above the rest of the line; *Split* on a longer line asks *How many?* as well as how many ways. `ITEM_NOT_SHAREABLE` is no longer returned.

- Flaky on CI's WebKit, cause not confirmed (7 Nov): the Split and Not claimed sheets sometimes stay open after their last action. Both close in `startTransition`. It doesn't reproduce on a Mac. If `claims.spec.ts:303` (tagged `@webkit`) flakes again, look there first.
- Issue comments posted from Claude cloud sessions get a "Generated by Claude Code" footer added by the GitHub proxy; it can't be removed from there.
- Golden receipts (`apps/api/fixtures/receipts`, 11 of them, #69 closed): with prompt v10, 10 read to the penny with the right venue (`npm run golden -w apps/api`, or `-- --api https://api.test.paymeback.dev` from a cloud session; the API serves cached reads, so it shows a prompt change only after it's deployed). `themill` sometimes splits its Peppercorn line differently. `thebank` (a 216×450 photo) misreads three prices and flags none as unsure; Review bill still shows *receipt says £92.00*. The reader's own unsure notes are weakly calibrated, so Review bill now says *Doesn't add up* (how far off, *See photo*) whenever the lines don't make the printed total, and clears it once they do (#107).
- Nothing has been tried on a real phone with a real Bedrock read since the switch. Bedrock access (use-case form, Marketplace subscription) was completed on 1 Nov.
